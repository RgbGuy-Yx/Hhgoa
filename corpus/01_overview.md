# Hacker House Goa 2026 — Event Overview

Hacker House Goa 2026 is a five-day builder residency held on the coast of Goa from March 2 to March 6, 2026. It brings together 60 selected hackers, mentors, and founders to build ambitious projects in a shared house rather than a conference hall. The program is organized around focused build tasks rather than a single freeform hackathon theme.

Participants live and work together for the full duration of the event. Each day mixes unstructured build time with short mentor office hours, a communal dinner, and an evening demo or talk. The organizers describe the format as "high trust, low ceremony" — badges are optional, but working code is not.

Hacker House Goa is organized by the Goa Builders Collective in partnership with three regional angel networks. The venue is a converted heritage property in Assagao, Goa, with dedicated maker space, a recording studio for demo videos, and stable high-speed internet backed by a local generator for power redundancy.
