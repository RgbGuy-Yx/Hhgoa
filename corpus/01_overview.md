# Hacker House Goa 2026 - Event Overview

Hacker House Goa 2026 (HHGoa'26) is an elite builder residency organized by 2:47PM Studio (247pmstudio) in partnership with regional venture capital networks and Web3/AI ecosystem leaders. Held in Goa, India, the residency brings together a curated group of 247 high-caliber builders, AI engineers, designers, and startup founders under the motto "हैक नहीं, बनाओ" (Don't just hack, build!).

Unlike standard hackathons that focus on throwaway prototypes, Hacker House Goa is an intensive product residency designed to ship, launch, and scale production-ready systems. Selected builders live and work together in a collaborative beachfront resort environment with 24/7 dedicated maker spaces, power redundancy, high-speed fiber drops, and direct mentorship from tier-1 VCs and tech leads.

The total prize and startup grant pool stands at ₹45,00,000 (₹45 Lakhs), alongside direct venture acceleration and investor backing for top-performing teams. Registrations and shortlisting task submissions are hosted via Devfolio (hacker-house-goa-2026.devfolio.co).
